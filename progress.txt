2. **Simplicity wins** — 400 lines of orchestration, 3 npm packages
3. **Testing wins** — 79/79 tests, 30/30 sample gold, safety 1.000
4. **Evidence wins** — Same-user only, top-2, oldest first
5. **Interview wins** — Specific, auditable, honest answers
6. **AWS knowledge helps mentally** but not for implementation (hackathon runs locally)
7. **DevOps background is the real differentiator** — production thinking, observability, reliability
